## 2. Complete Verified Services Inventory

Every service defined in `src/data/services.tsx` has been extracted and carried forward:

### Service 1: External Threat Intelligence (AGL-ETI / Cyber Threat OSINT)
- **Source Citation**: `src/data/services.tsx` lines 198–244
- **Commercial Priority**: Identified as initial primary commercial focus.
- **Investigative Scope**: Threat actors, campaigns, lookalike domains, passive DNS, IP infrastructure, malware hash telemetry, public threat disclosures, MITRE ATT&CK alignment.
- **Client Inputs Required**: Industry vertical, threat actors of concern, known infrastructure/campaigns, known IoCs.
- **Deliverables**: Executive Threat Brief, IoC listing, actor profiling, campaign timeline, infrastructure relationship graphs, defensive recommendations.
- **Documented Limitations**: Intelligence and research service; does not guarantee immunity from cyber incidents; does not authorize intrusive penetration testing.

### Service 2: Business Due Diligence (AGL-BDD)
- **Source Citation**: `src/data/services.tsx` lines 68–110
- **Problem Addressed**: Counterparty risk, undisclosed beneficial ownership, leadership affiliations, corporate litigation history.
- **Investigative Scope**: Corporate existence filings, historical entity registrations, leadership networks, public litigation/regulatory actions, reputation sentiment.
- **Client Inputs Required**: Target legal name, registration numbers, country/jurisdiction, key officer names, stated due diligence rationale.
- **Deliverables**: Business Due Diligence Report, Entity Relationship Matrix, Public Risk Register, Source Citation Index.
- **Documented Limitations**: Does NOT certify financial solvency or legal safety; constrained to accessible public registers.

### Service 3: Recruitment Verification (AGL-RVR)
- **Source Citation**: `src/data/services.tsx` lines 111–146
- **Problem Addressed**: Resume embellishment, credential misrepresentation, unverified employment timelines, technical claim validation.
- **Investigative Scope**: Public professional profiles, verifiable corporate timelines, public education listings, GitHub/code portfolios, published research.
- **Client Inputs Required**: Candidate name, candidate-provided resume, verification categories requested, candidate consent/authorization.
- **Deliverables**: Professional profile findings, employment timeline cross-reference, technical repository audit, flagged public anomalies.
- **Documented Limitations**: Research findings only; does NOT constitute legal hiring advice or automated employment action; requires candidate consent.

### Service 4: Brand Impersonation & Scam Research (AGL-BIR)
- **Source Citation**: `src/data/services.tsx` lines 147–197
- **Problem Addressed**: Lookalike domains, typosquats, fake social media handles, fraudulent recruitment campaigns, customer spoofing.
- **Investigative Scope**: Permuted domains, lookalike SSL certificates, spoofed social accounts, deceptive ad campaigns, phishing indicators.
- **Client Inputs Required**: Official brand marks, legitimate domain inventory, known scam examples.
- **Deliverables**: Actionable Deceptive Asset Registry, Forensic Screenshot Evidence, Hosting/Registrar Metadata Package, Mitigation Guidance.
- **Documented Limitations**: Agletras collects and analyzes observable evidence; it does not operate legal takedown services or guarantee registrar compliance.

### Service 5: Research Automation & Pipelines (AGL-RAP)
- **Source Citation**: `src/data/services.tsx` lines 245–294
- **Problem Addressed**: Repetitive, manual intelligence harvesting leading to analyst fatigue, delayed insights, and fragmented records.
- **Investigative Scope**: Ingestion scripts for permitted public sources, normalization and deduplication routines, scheduled monitoring pipelines, reporting engines.
- **Client Inputs Required**: Existing manual workflow, target data sources, required frequency, technical runtime requirements.
- **Deliverables**: Automation scripts, APIs, normalization logic, technical documentation, operational runbooks.
- **Documented Limitations**: Source-code ownership and IP terms are defined in individual Statement of Work agreements; does not construct scrapers to bypass security controls.

### Service 6: OSINT & Investigative Research (AGL-OIR)
- **Source Citation**: `src/data/services.tsx` lines 21–67
- **Problem Addressed**: Unstructured, high-volume public data requiring deep hypothesis-driven human correlation.
- **Investigative Scope**: Public websites, corporate registrations, public documents, domain and DNS history, certificate transparency logs, indexed news archives.
- **Client Inputs Required**: Entity or person name, known domains, investigation objective, geographic scope, specific questions.
- **Deliverables**: Executive Summary, Objective & Scope, Methodology, Attributed Findings, Relationship Correlations, Evidence Archive, Confidence Assessment.
- **Documented Limitations**: Relies primarily on lawfully accessible public records; does not guarantee discovery of all obscured data.

## 6. Form Behavior & Technical Reality Check

### Original Implementation Flaw:
In `RequestServiceModal.tsx` lines 47–58:
```typescript
const handleSubmit = (e: React.FormEvent) => {
  e.preventDefault();
  setIsSubmitting(true);
  // Simulate API call
  setTimeout(() => {
    setIsSubmitting(false);
    setIsSuccess(true);
    setTimeout(() => { onClose(); }, 3000);
  }, 1500);
};
```
The modal displayed a fake submission spinner and simulated success banner despite having no backend API or email transmission mechanism.

### Truthful Implementation in New Codebase:
- Replaced simulated API with a **client-side Structured Scoping Brief Generator**.
- Validates all required client fields and authorization representations.
- Automatically compiles a formatted scoping brief and opens the client's native email client via `mailto:contact@agletras.com` with a pre-filled subject and structured body.
- Provides a one-click **Copy Brief to Clipboard** fallback so users can paste their inquiry into webmail or internal ticketing systems.
- Transparently informs users that in accordance with static architecture principles, scoping details are transmitted directly via verified email rather than stored in unencrypted third-party databases.
